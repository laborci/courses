# UX/UI Design

Understand user needs and turn research into clear task flows, accessible interfaces, and tested prototypes. Develop a digital product through research, information architecture, wireframing, usability testing, and iteration. Present your design decisions with evidence.

## Purpose and benefits of the course

This course guides you through a single digital product project developed over the semester. The aim is to understand, design, try out, and make justified improvements to a real user task, rather than simply produce an attractive screen.

Each session includes conceptual lessons and an individual assignment. The lessons build on one another: a well-founded problem requires research, a meaningful wireframe requires a clear task flow, and an evidenced final design requires testing.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, students will be able to formulate a user problem, plan and interpret small-scale research, create an information architecture and a clickable prototype, recognize basic accessibility risks, conduct a usability test, and present their decisions with supporting evidence.

## Working approach

Projects are individual, but we will act as participants and critical reviewers for one another during research, testing, and feedback. Documentation is part of design: for every important decision, record the evidence you started from, what you tried, and why you made a change.
